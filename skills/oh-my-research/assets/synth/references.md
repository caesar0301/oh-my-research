# References

Use one consistent reader-facing citation style throughout the report.

## Author–date example

Smith, J., & Chen, L. (2025). Title of the study. *Journal Name, 12*(3), 10–24. https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx

张伟、李明（2025）。《研究题目》。《期刊名称》，12（3），10–24。https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx

## Numbered example

[1] J. Smith and L. Chen, “Title of the study,” *Journal Name*, vol. 12, no. 3, pp. 10–24, 2025. https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx

Do not include internal material IDs, private file paths, workflow metadata, or incomplete placeholder citations.
